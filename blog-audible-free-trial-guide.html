The #1 Audible Question Answered

Do you really get to keep your audiobook if you cancel your trial?
YES. 100% YES. Any audiobook you purchase with your free trial credit is permanently yours. It stays in your free Audible account indefinitely, and you can re-download or listen to it whenever you want via iOS, Android, or Alexa, even years after canceling.

Additionally, you receive unlimited access to the entire Audible Plus catalog (thousands of included audiobooks and podcasts) during your 30-day trial period.

1. What Exactly Comes with the 30-Day Trial?

2. Pro Tips for Maximizing Your Free Credit

Since your trial credit can purchase any audiobook in Audible's massive collection, here is how seasoned listeners get the highest possible monetary value:

  • Pick High-Priced Audiobooks: Don't waste your credit on an $8 novella. Use it on comprehensive bestsellers, memoirs narrated by authors, or dense non-fiction titles that normally retail for $25.00 to $40.00.
  • Check the Narration: Look for titles narrated by celebrity voice actors or full-cast dramatizations (e.g., The Sandman, Project Hail Mary, Born a Crime).
  • Take Advantage of the Great Listen Guarantee: If you use your credit on a book and find that the narrator's voice doesn't suit your taste after a few chapters, Audible allows you to exchange it for another title with no hassle.

4. How to Cancel in Under 60 Seconds at $0.00

Amazon and Audible make account management completely digital with no need to call customer service:

  1. Open your web browser and go to your Account Details on Audible.com or Amazon.
  2. Under Membership Details, click "Cancel Membership".
  3. Follow the prompt to confirm cancellation.
  4. Your account immediately reverts to a free status. Your downloaded credit audiobook remains accessible forever in your library!
