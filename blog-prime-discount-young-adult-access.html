The Big Savings Summary

If you are between 18 and 24 years old (or an enrolled college student), you can get a 6-month free trial, followed by 50% off ($7.49/mo or $69/yr). If you receive SNAP/EBT, Medicaid, SSI, or WIC, you qualify for Prime Access at just $6.99/mo with a 30-day free trial.

Both tiers provide identical Core benefits to standard $139/year Prime: same-day delivery, full Prime Video catalog, ad-free music, and member-only Prime Day deals.

Formerly limited solely to 4-year university students, Amazon expanded the program under Prime for Young Adults. Today, any individual between the ages of 18 and 24 is eligible, whether attending trade school, community college, university, or currently working.

3. Prime Access: $6.99/mo for Qualifying Assistance

Prime Access is Amazon's dedicated initiative to ensure essential online grocery delivery and household necessities remain affordable for low-income households.

At $6.99 per month, members save more than 50% compared to standard monthly Prime, with no long-term contracts. You can pause or cancel at any time.

Qualifying Programs for Prime Access:

  • SNAP / EBT: Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program cards.
  • Medicaid: Including state-administered Medicaid cards.
  • WIC: Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children.
  • SSI: Supplemental Security Income.
  • TANF: Temporary Assistance for Needy Families.
  • NSLP: National School Lunch Program letters.
  • LIHEAP: Low-Income Home Energy Assistance Program.

4. How to Verify Your Eligibility (Step-by-Step)

  1. For Young Adults (18–24): Click the Young Adult portal. Enter your birthdate, state ID, or university .edu email. Verification is typically instantaneous through SheerID.
  2. For Prime Access: Visit the official Prime Access registration page. Take a photo of your EBT card or benefits statement and submit it. Most submissions are verified within 15 minutes.
  3. Activate Your Trial: Enjoy your free trial period immediately while having full access to Prime Video, Music, and free delivery.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pay with SNAP EBT on Amazon? Yes! SNAP EBT cardholders can use their benefits to buy eligible groceries directly on Amazon.com, Amazon Fresh, and Whole Foods Market.
How long does the 50% discount last? Young Adult membership remains valid until your 25th birthday (or up to 4 years for college students). Prime Access requires simple re-verification every 12 months.
