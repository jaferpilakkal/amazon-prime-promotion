Audiobook Terminology & Key Definitions

An Audible pre-order is a reservation feature that secures upcoming audiobook titles before publication and automatically delivers them on release day.

An Audible credit is a monthly token that redeems any audiobook in Audible's catalog regardless of retail dollar price.

A full-cast audio drama refers to an audio production featuring distinct voice actors for each character with sound design.

An audio embargo is the official publication date and time before which a licensed audiobook recording cannot be distributed to the public.

A Whispersync audiobook is an Amazon feature that synchronizes reading progress between Kindle e-books and Audible audio tracks.
